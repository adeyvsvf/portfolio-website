## Technical Details

The hero section slider navigation arrows were previously positioned at `left: 60px` and `right: 60px`, which placed them directly on top of the slider content. This made them difficult to see depending on the content of the image or video.

The fix repositioned them to `left: -25px` and `right: -25px`, moving them outside the content area. Additionally, the styling was updated to use theme variables for better consistency with the overall design.

Responsive adjustments were also added to ensure proper positioning on mobile devices, with the arrows being positioned at `left: -15px` and `right: -15px` on screens smaller than 768px.
